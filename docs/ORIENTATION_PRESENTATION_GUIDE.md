## Slide 11: How to Access & Install (Web & Mobile PWA)
### 📌 Slide Content (Bullet Points)
* **Step 1: Sign In**
  * Go to the portal URL on any device.
  * Tap **“Continue with CJC Google Account”** using your official `@g.cjc.edu.ph` email.

  * Select your Degree Program (BSCoE, BSCE, BSECE) and Year Level.

  * **Android (Chrome):** Tap *Install App* or menu (⋮) → *Add to Home Screen*.

## Quick Presenter Tips for Orientation Success
1. **Live Demo Transition:** After Slide 6, switch to a live browser tab for 60 seconds to click a live receipt and let attendees see it pop up on screen.
2. **Emphasize Security:** Remind students that their Google login keeps their personal data secure, and that public views are read-only so figures cannot be altered.
3. **Encourage Immediate PWA Installation:** Give the audience 2 minutes at Slide 11 to tap "Add to Home Screen" so everyone leaves the room with the app already installed.
